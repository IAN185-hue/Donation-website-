The Kiine Ward 2027 Blueprint

Why We Need Iano in the Assembly

Your campaign contribution directly powers ground mobilization, community civic engagement, logistics, and grassroots organizing across all polling stations in Kiine Ward.

Youth & Women Empowerment

Creating sustainable Ward bursary funds, vocational skills training, and micro-grant programs to boost local youth enterprises and women-led cooperatives in Kiine Ward.

Water & Infrastructure

Championing piped clean water distribution to every household, borehole installations, and lobbying for well-maintained feeder roads across Kirinyaga County.

Agricultural Growth

Securing subsidized agricultural inputs, subsidized fertilizers, cold storage facilities, and fair markets for Kiine Ward coffee, tea, and horticultural farmers.

Accountable Governance

100% transparent Ward Development Fund oversight with quarterly open town halls so residents directly participate in project selection.
